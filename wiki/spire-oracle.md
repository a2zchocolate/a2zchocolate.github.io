**Spire Oracle** (Chinese: 问问尖塔) is a fortune-telling game that answers any question with a single item from *Slay the Spire 2*: a card, relic, potion or event. The item is picked so that its name, read literally, works as the answer.

## Gameplay
The player types a question in English or Chinese. The Spire replies with one item and a short reading that ties the name to the question, usually with a piece of advice.

| Question | The Spire answers |
|---|---|
| 明天期末考 (Final exam tomorrow) | 末日降临 (*End of Days*) |
| 喜欢爸爸还是妈妈 (Dad or mum?) | 双持 (*Dual Wield*) |
| 他还爱我吗 (Does he still love me?) | 相信着你 (*Believe in You*) |

The deck holds more than 1,100 cards, relics, potions and events, all with their official English and Chinese names.
