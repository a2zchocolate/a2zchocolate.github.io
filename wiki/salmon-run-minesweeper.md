**Salmon Run Minesweeper** (Chinese: 鲑鱼跑x扫雷) is a version of the classic Minesweeper puzzle themed on Salmon Run, the co-op mode of Nintendo's *Splatoon* series. The player collects Golden Eggs while avoiding the Small Fry hidden on the board.

## Gameplay
The rules are those of Minesweeper, with Salmon Run names:

| Minesweeper | Salmon Run Minesweeper |
|---|---|
| Safe cell | Golden Egg |
| Mine | Small Fry |
| Flag | Egg Basket |

The player reveals cells by tapping or clicking them; a number shows how many Small Fry are next to that cell. Egg Baskets mark suspected Small Fry, either by right-clicking or by switching to flag mode on a phone. The first tap is always safe. Revealing every cell without a Small Fry wins the round; uncovering a Small Fry ends it.

There are two difficulties:

| Difficulty | Board | Small Fry |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | 9 × 9 | 10 |
| Hard | 12 × 12 | 23 |

## Leaderboard
Players who clear a board can save their time under a name of their choice to a global leaderboard, with a separate ranking for each difficulty.
